Proof Round

In the Proof Round, participants are presented with several mathematical statements and must construct a rigorous proof to show that each statement is always true. Unlike standard problem-solving questions, answers must include clear logical reasoning, justified steps, and proper mathematical arguments rather than just a final result.

Difficulty: Insane
